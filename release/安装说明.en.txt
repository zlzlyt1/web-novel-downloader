Verification
Use SHA256SUMS.txt in this folder to verify downloaded files.

2.2.8 feature
“Accent color” is now named “function color” beside control border and fill. It colors expand arrows, reader states, page arrows, and downloader primary actions; border and fill colors also apply to downloader controls.

2.2.7 feature
Horizontal pages now keep ordinary paragraphs and content blocks together whenever they fit on one page; oversized blocks can still flow safely. The floating `<` and `>` arrows are centered. In Settings → Theme, light, sepia, and dark themes each provide separately saved custom control border, fill, and accent colors.

2.2.6 feature
Horizontal book pages now animate while turning. Circular outlined side buttons with `<` and `>` arrows appear during activity and hide automatically.

2.2.5 feature
Theme, page layout, format conversion, typography, and page-key controls are now grouped under an animated expandable Settings panel in the reader.

2.2.4 feature
The top bar now switches between vertical scrolling and horizontally paginated book reading. In horizontal mode, the wheel and customized page keys move one window-sized page at a time; arrow keys remain chapter navigation.

2.2.3 feature
Left and right arrow keys now turn pages and cross chapter boundaries. Existing chapter buttons and customizable page-key bindings remain available.

2.2.2 feature
PageUp and PageDown now turn pages and cross chapter boundaries. Customize the previous-page and next-page keys from the reader typography panel.

2.2.1 fix
Reading progress now stores the current chapter and in-chapter scroll position in AppData and survives portable-app restarts and upgrades. Existing local-storage progress is migrated when available.

2.2.0 feature
The reader opens EPUB, Markdown, and TXT books and converts the current book to either of the other formats while preserving its title, chapters, and text.

2.1.8 feature
Chapter updates now live in the downloader. Each downloaded book shows its current chapter count; enter a larger target and append only unseen chapters in that range while preserving existing text.

2.1.7 fix
Typography values now update immediately under the correct row. Font size, line spacing, paragraph spacing, and page margin no longer display the previous click's value.

2.1.6 features
Balanced and full-width modes are now one paragraph optimization switch. It reflows sentence-per-paragraph mobile TXT exports for desktop windows while preserving chapter headings, dialogue, speech cues, metadata, and scene breaks. Numbered chapter headings and duplicated table-of-contents entries are also handled.

2.1.4 features
The reader now includes a persistent bookshelf, incremental chapter updates, and duplicate download detection.

Security note
The binaries are not signed with a commercial code-signing certificate. Windows may display “Unknown publisher”.
